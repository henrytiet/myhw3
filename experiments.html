Why couldn't developers use MathML until recently?

Using caniuse.com/mathml, I noticed that there were long periods of time in which MathML was not supported by major browsers like Chrome and Edge up until 2022. Other browsers like Opera, Internet Explorer, and numerous mobile based browsers still do not to this day support MathML. This could definitely point towards why MathML was not used by developers for some time.
